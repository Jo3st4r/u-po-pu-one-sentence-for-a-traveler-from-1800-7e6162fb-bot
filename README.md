One sentence — 169 characters — explaining the modern internet to a traveller from 1800, for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-po-pu-one-sentence-for-a-traveler-from-1800-7e6162fb).

## The sentence

> In every house stands a glass box that speaks in the voices of strangers, shows moving pictures, and answers any question at once with a letter from a man you never met.

1 glass box, 1,000 voices, 0 letters ever posted: no word in it was coined after 1800 — glass, a box, voices, pictures, a letter — yet it carries the whole internet in one picture a traveller could hold in his head. No list of features, no modern word, just a house, a box, and the world writing back at once.
